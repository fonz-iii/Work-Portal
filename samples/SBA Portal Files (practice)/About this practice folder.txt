Practice copy of a portal folder. Everything here is either synthetic or public (BSP ranking pages).

To try it: in the portal, click "Choose folder" in the top bar and pick THIS folder.
The portal adds an Outputs folder inside each project folder and a READ ME file, then:
  - Employee Info loads the practice phone directory and Code of Conduct by itself;
  - QR Ph P2B Biller Directory fills in the Sept 30 masterlist (this period) and Sept 15 (previous);
  - Industry Ranking fills in 2026-Q2 (this quarter) and 2026-Q1 (previous quarter, with the old workbook).
Your real folder (e.g. Documents\SBA Portal Files) works the same way. Never put real files in GitHub.
